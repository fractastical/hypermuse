Sphere motion:
The spin is applied to the whole scene and the lane spheres sit off-centre, so it swings them out of frame instead of turning them in place. Off by default; turning it off also snaps them back to square.
Sphere outlines are the faint wireframe shells around each lane. Their brightness is re-set every frame per mode, so use these rather than expecting one nudge to hold.
Clips (click to play):
Every clip in the loaded set. Click one to cut to it now, which overrides the folder and colour filters. Dimmed rows are ones auto-advance would skip.
Fibonacci mosaic (triangle vids):
Per-tile content. auto keeps the alternating default; pick an effect or the moon to pin a tile. Tiles are rebuilt on apply, so they all restart. The orbit gifs field feeds every tile set to hypermoon + orbit gifs.
Folder loops (playlist):
One row per immediate subfolder of loops/ when the manifest has "folderLoopRoot":"loops" or per-clip loopGroup. Tick to include that folder's clips in auto-advance; untick to skip.
Color board (playlist filter):

Color cube board (tiled rows):
One horizontal band per color family (black, silver, gray, …). Cubes come from npm run build:color:cubes plus live capture from the playing loop.

Poem screen (second projector):
The poem on its own surface, beside the moon. Sized for a projection rather than a monitor: fewer lines at once means bigger type, and the readout below tells you how far back it reads. Put this window on the second output and leave the moon on the first.

free running
Tap four or more beats along to the music and every screen is then given a whole number of them, however long its own lines are — the hold absorbs the difference, and the typing is only hurried if the words would not otherwise land in time.
Keys, here and in the poem window itself (hold shift for a bigger step). The arrows walk the poem word by word, so pausing with space and driving it by hand keeps it with a reading:
→ ← a word on / back, rolling into the next or previous screen at either end · space pause · home restart · r t type slower / faster · 1–9 speed straight off · - = hold shorter / longer · 0 pace back to its defaults · pgdn pgup a whole screen, which is what a presenter's clicker sends · b tap the beat, shift+b drop it · , . fewer / more beats a screen
