ANNALS CURATOR

Click a photo to choose it for the public annals. Nothing is published unless you choose it, so an empty page publishes nothing. best of day leads that day full width, above the account, one per day. 18+ holds one back: it stays chosen, but the ordinary build leaves it out and only --explicit puts it in. Shift-click a clip to watch it without choosing it. Captions are optional and travel with the choice. Choices save in this browser as you make them — press download annals-curation.json and save it into data/hermes/ to hand them to the publisher.
