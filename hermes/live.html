About the Art Car

Return of Hermes is a roaming playa art car focused on connection, movement, and shared moments across the city and deep playa.

This page lets guests find the current Hermes position and send pickup or live act requests.

Off playa, Hermes is on Instagram as @hermesartcar, and the week just gone is written up in the annals.
